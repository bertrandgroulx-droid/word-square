How to play

  • Fill the grid so every row and every column spells a word.
  • Blue letters are given. The tray holds exactly the letters you need — no spares.
  • Tap a square, then tap a tray letter or type one. Tap a placed letter to send it back.
  • A dot lights up beside each row and under each column once it spells a word.
  • Any valid square wins, not just the one we had in mind.
  • 🎲 deals a practice puzzle; the label up top brings you back to today's.

Easy and Hard

  • Easy grids are mirrors. The first column spells the same word as the top row, the second column the same as the second row, and so on — so every row you solve hands you a column for free.
  • Hard grids have no mirror. All eight words (ten at 5×5) are different, and nothing comes free.

A new puzzle every day, at each size and difficulty. Your place is kept on this device, so you can put it down and come back.
